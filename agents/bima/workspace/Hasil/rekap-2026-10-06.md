Sumber: `inbox/pesanan-hari-ini.csv`

Perintah skrip (angka di bawah hasil skrip, bukan kira-kira):
```sh
python3 -c "
import csv, collections
p='inbox/pesanan-hari-ini.csv'
tot=collections.Counter(); cnt=collections.Counter(); n=0
with open(p) as f:
  for r in csv.DictReader(f):
    v=int(r['qty'])*int(r['harga'])
    tot[r['status']]+=v; cnt[r['status']]+=1; n+=1
print('total_order:',n)
print('cnt:',dict(cnt))
print('tot:',dict(tot))
print('omzet_lunas:',tot['lunas'])
print('pending:',tot['pending'])
print('batal:',tot['batal'])
"
```
